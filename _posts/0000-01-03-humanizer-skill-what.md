title: "When to use it"
---

- The text reads like a chatbot wrote it
- You're publishing a blog post, README, or LinkedIn note and want a real human voice
- You're auditing a document for AI tells before shipping
- You want a 0-100 score quantifying how AI-flagged the text reads
- You want the skill to edit a Markdown file in place, not just print a rewrite

North star: **LLMs regress to the statistical mean. Humans are weird, specific, and inconsistent. Write like a human.**
